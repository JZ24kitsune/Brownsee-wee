Every Paw Has a Story

My journey began in Ireland, far away from the home I know today. I was transported all the way to Singapore, where a new chapter of my life was waiting to begin.

One day, my future owner's father chose me from a pet shop and brought me home. That was the beginning of my life with my family!

Today, I live happily in Singapore, surrounded by the people who love me. Ireland was where my journey began, but Singapore is where I have made my home.

And my adventures are only beginning! ❤️
