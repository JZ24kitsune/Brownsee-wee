My Story 🌍

From Ireland to Singapore

Hello! My name is Brownsee Wee. My journey began in Ireland, far away from my home today.

I travelled all the way to Singapore and arrived at a pet shop, hoping to find a family of my own. One day, my owner's father chose me and brought me home.

That was the beginning of a wonderful new chapter! Today, I live happily in Singapore, surrounded by my family and the love that makes a house a home.

My journey brought me across the world, but my heart found a home in Singapore. ❤️
